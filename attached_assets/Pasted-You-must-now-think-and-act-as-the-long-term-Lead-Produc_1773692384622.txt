==================================================
PRODUCT VISION
==================================================

This platform is meant to become an AI-powered end-to-end carbon project operating system.

It should eventually allow a user to do things like:

- "Generate a carbon project for distributing 100,000 improved cookstoves in Burundi using TPDDTEC."
- "Which African country is most promising for a new cookstove project?"
- "Which standard should I choose: Verra or Gold Standard?"
- "What documents and steps do I need before I can even draft the PDD?"
- "Who should I invite to the local stakeholder consultation and how do I document it?"
- "What are the likely findings a validator will raise?"
- "Can you help me prepare the monitoring data and random sampling?"
- "Can you connect this project to a human expert reviewer?"
- "Can you compare my project to similar registered projects?"
- "Can you help me prepare for validation, monitoring, issuance, and buyers?"

The platform should become a true one-stop carbon project development platform:
- design projects
- benchmark markets
- choose methodologies
- estimate ERs
- write documents
- prepare validation
- manage monitoring
- help with operations
- optionally escalate to human experts
